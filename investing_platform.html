10-Week Curriculum
Your Learning Roadmap
Four structured stages. Expand each to see topics, examples, and common mistakes.
01
Financial Foundations
Time value · Compound interest · Inflation · Risk · Emergency fund
Weeks 1–2
▾
Time Value of Money
Why ₹100 today is worth more than ₹100 next year. The core principle behind every financial decision.
~8 min read
Compound Interest
₹10,000 at 12% CAGRCompound Annual Growth Rate — average annual growth rate over multiple years becomes ₹96,000 in 20 years — not ₹24,000. The most powerful force in investing.
~10 min read
Inflation
At 6% inflation, today's ₹100 buys only ₹31 worth of goods in 20 years. You must beat inflation to build real wealth.
~7 min read
Risk vs Return
Higher return always means higher risk. No exceptions. Anyone promising 30% guaranteed returns is lying.
~6 min read
Emergency Fund First
3–6 months of expenses in a liquid savings account before investing a single rupee. Non-negotiable.
~5 min read
Why this comes first: Without these 5 ideas, every financial decision you make rests on a shaky foundation. They explain 80% of all investing outcomes.
02
Investment Products
FD · Mutual funds · Index funds · SIP · Stocks · Gold · PPF
Weeks 3–5
▾
Fixed Deposits & RD
Safest starting point. ~7% returns, capital protection. Learn this first to understand the risk-return baseline.
~6 min read
Mutual Funds Basics
What they are, how NAVNet Asset Value — price of one unit of a mutual fund, calculated daily works, expense ratios, and the critical direct vs regular plan difference.
~12 min read
Index Funds
What Nifty 50 is. Why passive investing beats active management for most investors over 15+ years.
~10 min read
SIP Mechanics
Systematic Investment Plan — how rupee cost averaging works. ₹500/month over 20 years at 12% = ₹4.9 lakh. From ₹1.2 lakh invested.
~8 min read
Stocks Basics
What owning a share means. Market cap, P/E ratio. Don't invest in stocks without Stage 1 complete.
~11 min read
Gold as Asset
SGBs vs Gold ETF vs physical gold. Sovereign Gold Bonds (SGBs) are the best form — interest + price appreciation.
~7 min read
PPF & NPS
Government schemes for long-term and retirement. PPF gives EEE tax treatment — fully tax free at all stages.
~9 min read
Don't try to learn stocks before mutual funds. Master the simple products first — that alone puts you ahead of 90% of Indian retail investors.
03
Tax & Hidden Costs
STCG · LTCG · 80C · Expense ratio · Exit loads · STT
Weeks 6–7
▾
STCG vs LTCG
STCGShort-Term Capital Gains — profits from equity assets held less than 1 year. Taxed at 20%. on equity: 20%. LTCGLong-Term Capital Gains — profits from equity assets held over 1 year. Taxed at 12.5% over ₹1.25L per year. on equity: 12.5% over ₹1.25 lakh. When you sell matters as much as what you buy.
~10 min read
Section 80C
₹1.5 lakh deduction limit. ELSS funds, PPF, LIC, home loan principal all fall here. ELSS gives best returns among these.
~8 min read
Expense Ratio Impact
1% vs 0.1% expense ratio difference destroys 40%+ of returns over 20 years. Always choose direct plans.
~6 min read
Exit Loads
Penalty fees for redeeming mutual funds too early. Typically 1% if redeemed within 1 year. Plan your exit date.
~5 min read
STT & Brokerage
Securities Transaction Tax and broker fees erode trading returns. Use a discount broker for equity investing.
~5 min read
Dividend Taxation
Dividends are now added to your income and taxed at your slab rate. Not 10% flat anymore. Choose growth option.
~6 min read
Most beginners ignore tax until they sell. A wrong sell decision can cost 20–30% of your gains in tax. Learn this before your first redemption.
04
Portfolio Thinking
Asset allocation · Diversification · Behaviour · Rebalancing
Weeks 8–10
▾
Asset Allocation
How to split money between equity, debt, and gold based on your age and goals. Classic rule: 100 minus age in equity.
~9 min read
Diversification
What it actually means. Diversification protects against individual stock risk — it does NOT protect against market crashes.
~7 min read
Goal-Based Investing
Separate portfolios for house, retirement, education. Not one pot. Different time horizons need different products.
~8 min read
Behavioural Biases
FOMO, loss aversion, recency bias, overconfidence. The enemy is your own mind. Markets test your psychology.
~11 min read
Rebalancing
When and how to bring your portfolio back to target allocation. Typically once a year or when allocation drifts 5%+.
~7 min read
What to Ignore
Tips, hot stocks, TV analysts, "multibagger" claims, WhatsApp groups, influencers. All noise. All the time.
~5 min read
Most investors fail not because they picked wrong products — but because they panicked and sold at the wrong time. Behavioural finance is the most important stage.
